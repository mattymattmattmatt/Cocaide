Hole adds `on, count, pattern, prefix, suffix, tolerance`; balloon `member|body|item, on, shape: BALLOON_SHAPES, size, quantity, stack, arrow`; weld `weld?` (table) or `on` + `arrowSide/otherSide: WeldSymbolSide {symbol: WELD_SYMBOLS, size "a5"|"z7"|n, length, count, pitch, angle, root, contour, finish}`, `staggered, allAround, field, process, reference, more[]`; note `view?, leaders?: Leader[] {to: GeoRef|Vec2, via, arrow: ARROW_STYLES, spline}, height, bold, italic, underline, numbered, title, frame, angle`; centerMark `style: lines|cross|pitch`; `GdtAnnotation {rows: GdtRow[] {characteristic, diameter, spherical, tolerance, material M|L, modifiers: GDT_MODIFIERS, projected, unequal, per, datums: GdtDatumRef[]}, composite, on | dimension (+extension), at, count, above, below}`; `DatumAnnotation {label, on|dimension|gdt, at, open}`; `DatumTargetAnnotation`; `SurfaceAnnotation {symbol: SURFACE_SYMBOLS, values, process, lay: LAY_SYMBOLS, allowance, allAround, on|dimension, general}`; Leader/Text/Line/Sketch/RevisionCloud. `Drawing` adds `precision?`, `dualUnits?`. - Auto-dimension v2 (src/drafting/autodim.ts `autoDimension(input) → {annotations, unneeded}`, `holeSignature`), used by planDrawing for parts: overall sizes once (round parts: Ø from the side view), alike holes one callout with count, circular patterns EQ SP ON PCD, positions from @left/@bottom or centre to centre when symmetric (ordinate ≥5, "n× pitch" rows), pockets/slots sized and placed, outline radii TYP, fillets/chamfers (dimension or note), centre marks, datums `datum_A/B/C`, `general_notes` (numbered note placed bottom left above tables). An unused projected view (e.g. a plate's right view) is left out. Weldments: overall sizes, item lengths that are no overall size (after balloons/welds), welds, general notes.
- Checks: overall size accepts Ø dims; hole check accepts grouped callouts.
- Gaps: ISO 3098 lettering uses Liberation Sans (no stroke font); section views can't be auto-dimensioned beyond their sides (no edge keys at plan time); DXF has no DIMENSION entities (lines/text on DIMENSION/ANNOTATION layers); mitre angle dims on weldments not auto-added; `rememberPlaces` is not yet called by the app on each edit (Q2B/Q3A should call it after drawing edits).
- Editor wiring (after merging Q2B): src/ui/drawing/tools/annotationKinds.ts registers gdt, datum, datumTarget, surface, cosmeticThread, revisionCloud, leader, text, line, sketch with `registerAnnotationKind` (panels from the engine schemas flattened to paths: `tolerance.kind`, `rows.0.characteristic`, `arrowSide.symbol` …; drag moves `at` or `pts`) and extends Q2B's dimension/hole/balloon/weld/note/centerMark panels with the new fields. The Auto Dimension tool (and prop-view-autodim, ctx-view-autodim) now runs `autoDimensionSheetView(doc, sheet, viewId, measurements, nextId)` (src/drafting/autodim.ts): what the v2 engine would put in that view and it lacks.
